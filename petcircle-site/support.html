Support

We're a small team and read every message.

Need help? Email us at apppetcircle@gmail.com. Tell us what happened and which iPhone and iOS version you're using, and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password", enter your email, and follow the link or code we send. You can also change it any time in Profile > Change password.

How do I add friends?

Go to My Circle and add friends with their friend code or email. They'll get a request to accept. Only accepted friends can see your pets and posts.

How do I plan a trip and find a sitter?

Open the Trips tab, tap Plan a trip, pick your dates and which pets need care, then ask friends for help. They sign up for days, and you approve who covers each one. Sitters get your care sheet and any getting-in details, and send a report after each visit.

Can someone travel with me and manage the trip?

Yes. On your trip, tap + under Travelling with to add a friend. Once they join, they can manage the trip with the same abilities as you, except deleting it.

Why does Pet Circle ask for location?

Only to record walks. When you start a live walk we measure distance and draw your route. If you allow it, tracking continues while your phone is locked, until you finish the walk. You can change this in iPhone Settings > Pet Circle > Location.

How do I block or report someone?

Tap the ••• menu on their profile, post or message and choose Block or Report. We review every report.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile > Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, pets, posts, stories, walks, photos, messages and friendships.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are on in Profile > Notifications and in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Pet Circle. If it still doesn't work, email us and we'll help.

Safety and reports

To report a person or content, use the ••• menu in the app. For urgent safety concerns about a pet, contact a veterinarian or emergency animal hospital directly. In the US, the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center is at (888) 426-4435.
