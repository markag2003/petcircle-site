Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Pet Circle ("we", "the app") helps people care for their pets together. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect what you add to the app so it can work, we never sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and sites.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, password (stored securely by our provider, never visible to us), display name and profile photo.
  • Content you add: pets and their details, feeding times, reminders, health and care notes, trips and visits (including visit reports, photos, and the optional "getting in" details an owner enters for sitters), posts, stories, comments, messages, photos and videos, walks (including the duration, distance, route, an optional photo, and a place name), and your public profile text.
  • Your connections: your friends, friend requests, chats and groups you join, travel partners you add to a trip, and people you block.
  • Reports: when you report content or a person, we store the report so we can review it.
  • Notification information: a push notification token for your device and your notification settings.
  • Location: when you record a live walk, Pet Circle uses your device's location to measure distance and draw your route, and saves that route and the starting place with the walk. If you allow it, this continues while a walk you started is running, even if you lock your phone or switch to another app, and iOS shows an indicator while it does. Tracking stops when you finish or discard the walk. If you log a walk by hand, you can optionally tag a place. We don't collect your location when you aren't recording a walk.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your pets, reminders, trips, posts and messages to you and the people you choose to share with.
  • To send notifications you've turned on, such as friend requests, likes, comments, trip invites and visit updates.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our rules, and act on abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

Who can see your content

Posts are visible to your accepted friends and to the chats and groups you choose. Stories disappear from the feed after 24 hours. Your public profile (name, photo, short bio and place) is visible to your friends. People can find you by your exact email address unless you switch off "Let people find me" in Profile > Privacy. Your email address is never shown to other users. Walks, including their routes, are visible only to the pets' owners and to the friends, circles and groups you share them with.

A trip's care details and "getting in" notes are visible only to the trip's owner, any travel partners the owner adds, and sitters the owner has approved, and only until shortly after the trip ends.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores account data, content and files and handles sign-in.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • Apple provides the App Store, TestFlight and Apple's push notification service.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete my account). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, pets, posts, stories, walks, photos, messages and friendships. Expired stories and their files are removed automatically. Some records may remain briefly in backups.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your content at any time.
  • Turn notifications on or off in Profile > Notifications.
  • Control whether people can find you by email in Profile > Privacy.
  • Choose location access for walks in your iPhone's Settings > Pet Circle > Location.
  • Block or report anyone from the ••• menu.
  • Ask for a copy or correction of your data by emailing us.

Children

Pet Circle is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard protections, including encrypted connections and access rules that limit who can see each piece of content. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
