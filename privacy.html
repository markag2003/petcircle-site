Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Pet Circle ("we", "the app") helps people care for their pets together. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, password (stored securely by our provider, never visible to us), display name and profile photo.
  • Content you add: pets and their details, feeding times, reminders, health and care notes, trips and visits, posts, stories, comments, messages, photos and videos, walks (including the duration, an optional photo, and an optional place if you choose to tag a location), and your public profile text.
  • Your connections: your friends, friend requests, My Circle contacts you enter (names, phone numbers, emails, notes), chats and groups you join, and people you block.
  • Reports: when you report content or a person, we store the report so we can review it.
  • Notification information: a push notification token for your device and your notification settings.
  • Location: only when you choose to tag a walk's location. We don't track you in the background.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your pets, reminders, posts and messages to you and the people you choose to share with.
  • To send notifications you've turned on, such as friend requests, likes, comments and visit updates.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content that breaks our rules, and act on abuse.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

Who can see your content

Posts are visible to your accepted friends and to the chats and groups you choose. Stories disappear from the feed after 24 hours. Your public profile (name, photo, short bio and place) is visible to your friends. People can find you by your exact email address unless you switch off "Let people find me" in Profile > Privacy. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores account data, content and files and handles sign-in.
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • Apple provides the App Store, TestFlight and Apple's push notification service.

These providers process data on our behalf to run the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete my account). Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, pets, posts, stories, walks, photos, messages and friendships. Expired stories and their files are removed automatically. Some records may remain briefly in backups.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your content at any time.
  • Turn notifications on or off in Profile > Notifications.
  • Control whether people can find you by email in Profile > Privacy.
  • Block or report anyone from the ••• menu.
  • Ask for a copy or correction of your data by emailing us.

Children

Pet Circle is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use industry-standard protections, including encrypted connections and access rules that limit who can see each piece of content. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
