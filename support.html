Common questions

How do I add a friend? Open My Circle, tap Find people, and enter their email address, or share your friend link from the same screen.

How do I report or block someone? Tap the ••• on their post, story, comment, message or profile, then choose Report or Block. You can unblock people in Profile > Blocked people.

How do I delete my account? Go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and your content.

How do I stop notifications? Go to Profile > Notifications and switch off anything you don't want, or turn notifications off for Pet Circle in your iPhone settings.

Reporting a problem or abusive content. Reports made in the app are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also email us at the address above.
